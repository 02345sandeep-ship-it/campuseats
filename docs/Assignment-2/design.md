# CampusEats Design

## Task 1 — Capabilities
### 1. Manage Profile & Addresses

**Description:**  
Students can manage their profile and delivery addresses.
### 2. Browse Restaurants & Menus

**Description:**  
Students can browse campus restaurants and their menus.
### 3. Add to Cart & Place Order

**Description:**  
Students can add food items to a cart and place an order.
### 4. Pay / Refund

**Description:**  
The system handles online payments and refunds for orders.
### 5. Assign Rider & Track Delivery

**Description:**  
The system assigns a rider to deliver the food and allows the student to track the delivery.
### 6. Send Notifications

**Description:**  
The system sends notifications as the order moves through different stages such as order placed, paid, on the way, and delivered.
